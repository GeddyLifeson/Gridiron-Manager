## What this repository does to stay on the right side of those lines

- Teams, schools and leagues are generated and checked against a blocked-terms list of real marks (bible A6.2, `packages/football-content/data/world/blocked-terms.json`).
- The release gate (`npx tsx scripts/release/gate.ts`) scans the shipped tree for real marks, real people's names, research datasets and per-person records, and checks the asset licence register. A public release waits for those checks and for the legal review item (R1.2.10 item 5).
- Real people appear only through an import the user runs on their own copy (bible A6.3).
